# Exemplar corpus + distilled DNA

The single most underused asset: taste already distilled into one rule per site. Encode these
verbatim as builder constraints and floor-rubric lines. Screenshot the starred ones as vision
references for the diff-vs-reference check. The rest are the memory-library backlog.

## Premium-tech / product-led
- **Linear** ★ — show the real product output as the hero, not marketing.
- **Zed** ★ — dark-native by default; show real, even imperfect, output.
- **Tesla** — spacing and type as the only design language; zero noise.
- **Arc** — full-width product shots carried by a human narrative.
- **Raycast** — positioning by contrast: reframe what the tool is really for.

## Mass-affluent aspiration
- **Polestar** — confident CTAs, no urgency language.
- **Teenage Engineering** — function as the aesthetic; less but better.
- **Oura** — story told in use-case moments, not feature lists.
- **Stripe** — abstract product made concrete with metaphor and real narrative.

## Interactive craft (premium wow)
- **Tresmares Capital** ★ — capital firm done premium-minimal; one bold accent + concept metaphor. Closest to RM's world.
- **Exat** — motion that reveals one idea then pauses; scroll is state, not spectacle.
- **Lando Norris** ★ — one signature accent on black; tight fast easing.
- **Dropbox Brand** — operator content as an interactive product, not a static doc.
- **Symphony of Vines** — cinematic 3D that stays editorial, if going bold.

★ = seed vision references for the floor loop.
