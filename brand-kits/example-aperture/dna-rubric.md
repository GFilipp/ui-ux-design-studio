# DNA rubric — Aperture (example, second kit)

Craft rules are identical to every brand (see `example-rocketminds/dna-rubric.md`):
one accent used sparingly; type + space carry the design; product is the hero; no dead
zones; confident CTAs; one idea per line, no orphans; AA contrast; clean type first.

## [Aperture] brand
- [ ] Light-native, generous whitespace.
- [ ] Single blue accent; everything else neutral.
- [ ] Sans-serif throughout; plain, confident voice.
